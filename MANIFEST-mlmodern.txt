MANIFEST for the converted MLModern / MLModern Math fonts
=========================================================

These are modified versions of existing fonts, renamed as requested by the GUST
Font License 1.0 and as the LPPL 1.3c asks of modified works. They are not the
original fonts and are not endorsed by the original authors.

Every family is provided twice, as OpenType (otf/, CFF outlines) and as TrueType
(ttf/, quadratic outlines). The two folders are complete, parallel sets.

TEXT FONTS
----------
Derived from MLModern 1.2 (Daniel Benjamin Miller, 2021; LPPL 1.3c), itself
built on Latin Modern (B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki). Source: TeX Live 2026,
package mlmodern, 92 Type 1 files (.pfb), byte-identical to TeX Live 2023.
Modification: converted from Type 1 to OpenType and TrueType; name tables
rewritten so the faces form the families listed below. The 10pt design size was
used for every face. The Type 1 files carry no kerning or ligature data; see
"KERNING AND LIGATURES" below.

  Family                    Face          Derived from
  MLModern                  Regular       mlmr10
  MLModern                  Italic        mlmri10
  MLModern                  Bold          mlmbx10
  MLModern                  Bold Italic   mlmbxi10
  MLModern Sans             Regular       mlmss10
  MLModern Sans             Italic        mlmsso10
  MLModern Sans             Bold          mlmssbx10
  MLModern Sans             Bold Italic   mlmssbo10
  MLModern Mono             Regular       mlmtt10
  MLModern Mono             Italic        mlmtti10
  MLModern Math Letters     Regular       mlmmi10
  MLModern Math Letters     Bold          mlmmib10
  MLModern Math Symbols     Regular       mlmsy10
  MLModern Math Symbols     Bold          mlmbsy10
  MLModern Math Extension   Regular       mlmex10

Additional modification, TrueType text fonts only: the OS/2 table was upgraded
from version 1 to version 3 and given the x-height and cap-height values of the
OpenType twin, so applications size lines identically in both formats.

KERNING AND LIGATURES
---------------------
Added to the eight proportional faces (MLModern and MLModern Sans, all four
styles each), in both formats, as GPOS 'kern' and GSUB 'liga' features. Source:
TeX Live 2026 TFM files and Latin Modern's lm-ec.enc encoding vector (package
lm, same release).

  Face                       TFM file            Kern pairs
  MLModern Regular           ec-mlmr10.tfm       2484
  MLModern Italic            ec-mlmri10.tfm      3716
  MLModern Bold              ec-mlmbx10.tfm      2477
  MLModern Bold Italic       ec-mlmbxi10.tfm     3709
  MLModern Sans Regular      ec-mlmss10.tfm      2255
  MLModern Sans Italic       ec-mlmsso10.tfm     2255
  MLModern Sans Bold         ec-mlmssbx10.tfm    2255
  MLModern Sans Bold Italic  ec-mlmssbo10.tfm    2255

Ligatures: ff, fi, fl, ffi, ffl only. The TFM ligature rules that are TeX typing
conventions (hyphen and quote sequences) were not included. MLModern Mono and
the MLModern Math Letters/Symbols/Extension faces are unchanged.

MATH FONTS
----------
Derived from New Computer Modern Math 8.1.1 (Antonis Tsolomitis, 2019-2026;
GUST Font License 1.0). Source: TeX Live 2026, package newcomputermodern.

  Files                                      Derived from (source/)
  otf/MLModernMath-Regular.otf  (.ttf)       NewCMMath-Book.otf
  otf/MLModernMath-Bold.otf     (.ttf)       NewCMMath-Bold.otf

Modification of the OpenType files: name table and the CFF internal font names
rewritten to the family "MLModern Math". For the Bold file only, OS/2
fsSelection and head macStyle were corrected to mark it as Bold (upstream marks
it Regular). Glyph outlines, the MATH table, GSUB, GPOS, GDEF, hmtx, and the
cmap mappings are unchanged.

Modification of the TrueType files: made from the renamed OpenType files by
converting the cubic CFF outlines to quadratic TrueType outlines (maximum fit
tolerance 0.5 font units). Only the outline tables were replaced (CFF became
glyf and loca; maxp, post, head and the left side bearings in hmtx were adjusted
as TrueType requires; a gasp table was added). MATH, GSUB, GPOS, GDEF, OS/2,
cmap and name are byte-identical to the OpenType files, and the glyph order is
unchanged. In the Bold, glyph braceright.ex carries two zero-area single-point
contours upstream; TrueType cannot store these, so they are absent from the
TrueType file. The drawn shape is identical.

source/ holds the two unmodified source files, for provenance. They keep their
original names and should not be installed alongside the renamed fonts.
